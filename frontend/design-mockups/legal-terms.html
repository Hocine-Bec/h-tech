04Prices & payment

Every price shown on H-Tech is final, in Algerian dinars (DA), VAT included. Prices sync from our distributors up to 3 times per day and can change without notice.

When you reach checkout, we lock your price for 15 minutes. If the price changes during that window, we honor the locked price. After the window expires, the current price applies.

We accept cash on delivery only for consumer orders. For business orders above 500,000 DA, we accept bank transfer against a proforma invoice.

Cash inspection at door: You can open the box, verify the item is present, in its original packaging, and undamaged before paying the courier. If anything looks wrong, refuse the parcel and you owe nothing.

06Returns & warranty

Dead on arrival (DOA)

You have 7 days from delivery to open a return for a defective item. Requirements: original packaging, photos of the issue, and a description. Approved returns are refunded in full including the delivery fee, via cash reimbursement scheduled through the same courier (3–5 business days).

Change of mind

Cash on delivery gives you the equivalent of a change-of-mind window at the door: refuse the parcel and you owe nothing. After delivery, change-of-mind returns are accepted only for sealed, unopened items within 48 hours and at our discretion.

Manufacturer warranty

Every product ships with its full manufacturer warranty (typically 2 years peripherals, 3 years GPU/motherboard/SSD, 5 years high-end memory). Warranty is honored directly by the manufacturer or its regional service partner. We help you file the claim; we do not act as the warranty provider.
