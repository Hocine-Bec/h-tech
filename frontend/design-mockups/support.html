Popular questions

Can I inspect the package before paying?

Yes. Open the box, check the item is present and undamaged, then pay the courier in cash. You can refuse if anything looks wrong.
How long does delivery take?

Algiers and surrounding wilayas: 1–2 business days. Other wilayas: 2–4 business days. Deep south (Tamanrasset, Adrar, Illizi): up to 6 days.
What if my item arrives dead or damaged?

You have 7 days from delivery to open a DOA return. We need photos of the issue and the original packaging. Approved returns are refunded in full.
The price changed between my cart and checkout — what now?

Prices sync from distributors 3× daily. If a price changes, we show a warning at checkout and re-lock it for 15 minutes so you can decide.
How does manufacturer warranty work?

Every product ships with its manufacturer warranty (usually 2–3 years). We help you file the claim directly with the brand — no middleman markup or delays.
