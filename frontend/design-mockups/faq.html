Returns & warranty

The 7-day DOA policy, warranty, and change-of-mind returns.

You have 7 days from delivery to open a Dead-on-Arrival (DOA) return. Go to your order page, click "Return this item," pick the reason, and upload photos of the issue (and the original packaging, if damaged).

Approved DOA returns are refunded in full including the delivery fee. Stock is restored to the catalog once we've received the item back.

Keep the original packaging. Manufacturers require it for DOA claims, and we pass that requirement through.

Every product ships with its full manufacturer warranty (typically 2 years for peripherals, 3 years for GPUs / motherboards / SSDs, 5 years for high-end memory). Your order confirmation email lists the warranty period per item.

For warranty claims after the 7-day DOA window, we help you file directly with the manufacturer — most brands (MSI, ASUS, Gigabyte, Corsair, Samsung, WD) have regional service partners we've coordinated with before.

Not by default. Our 7-day return window covers defective items only — cash-on-delivery gives you the equivalent of a change-of-mind window at the door: refuse the parcel and you owe nothing.

Exceptions: if an item is sealed and unopened, we may accept a change-of-mind return within 48h of delivery. WhatsApp us to arrange.

Physical damage caused after delivery, missing accessories that weren't part of the retail package, products used against manufacturer guidelines (e.g., DIY delidded CPUs, non-standard voltage), and any item outside the 7-day window.

Post-window issues fall under manufacturer warranty — we still help you file the claim.

From opening a return to cash back in your hands: 3–5 business days on average. Longer if we need to physically inspect the item (e.g., a GPU with an intermittent fault) — typically 7 days end-to-end.
